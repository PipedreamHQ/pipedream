# Overview

[Invoice Tariff](https://invoicetariff.com) is a free U.S. tariff toolkit for cross-border sellers and trade teams: an HTS code search covering 5,600+ codes with their current MFN general duty rates, layered landed-cost calculators (Section 301, Section 232, IEEPA and more), and the Tariff Radar — a continuously updated changelog of U.S. tariff and fee changes with effective dates and official sources. With the Invoice Tariff components on Pipedream, you can wire live U.S. tariff data into your quoting, compliance and monitoring workflows: resolve HTS codes for your products, watch for upcoming rate changes, and alert your team before a change hits your margins.

# Example Use Cases

- **Classify products before quoting**: In a pricing workflow, resolve each SKU to its 8-digit HTS code with the **Search HTS Codes** action, then pass the returned rate into your quote or landed-cost calculation.

- **Monitor tariff changes and alert the team**: Run **Get Latest Tariff Updates** on a schedule and deliver new Section 301, Section 232 or IEEPA changes to Slack or email, so sales and sourcing teams adjust prices before the effective date.

- **Enrich e-commerce catalogs**: Periodically pull the latest tariff updates to refresh duty estimates stored alongside product records, keeping displayed landed costs consistent with the latest U.S. rules.
